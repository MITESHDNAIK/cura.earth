# FAO Technical Manual: Recarbonizing Global Soils (2022)

**Source**: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)  
**Publication Year**: 2022  
**Key Topic**: Soil Organic Carbon Sequestration and Soil Biodiversity  

## Summary Findings
1. **Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) and Microbial Communities**:
   - Management practices that integrate deep-rooted perennial crops, agroforestry, and legume-based intercropping increase soil organic carbon by ~15% to 25% within 2 to 3 years.
   - Enhanced SOC serves as the energetic foundation for microbial biomass C, increasing fungal-to-bacterial biomass ratios and accelerating nutrient mineralization cycles.

2. **Hydrological Feedback**:
   - Every 1% increase in topsoil organic matter improves plant-available water holding capacity by up to 20,000 gallons per acre (approximately 187 m³ per hectare).
   - In semi-arid regions, this additional moisture retention dampens evaporative loss, mitigating high soil temperatures and preventing ecological collapse during intermittent dry spells.
