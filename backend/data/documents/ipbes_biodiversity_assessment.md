# IPBES Global Assessment Report on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services

**Source**: Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services (IPBES)  
**Publication Year**: 2022  
**Key Topic**: Habitat Fragmentation, Pollinators, and Agroecological Connectivity  

## Summary Findings
1. **Habitat Fragmentation & Pollinator Collapse**:
   - Agricultural expansion creating monoculture patches larger than 50 hectares without ecological corridors or hedgerows reduces native pollinator species richness by 50% to 75%.
   - Solitary bees and beneficial predator insects require continuous or stepped stepping-stone habitats within 300 meters of foraging zones.

2. **Connectivity Restoration**:
   - Reconnecting fragmented agricultural plots with native flowering hedgerows and riparian vegetative buffers yields a 45% increase in beneficial insect diversity within 2 years.
   - Restored pollinator richness correlates directly with a 15-20% boost in cross-pollinated crop yields without synthetic chemical augmentation.
